> Context for the week: last days in Hawaii, the flight back on the 30th, and then straight into the move-out. The shape of the week is the handoff from planning the trip to physically dismantling a life in New York.

## Day 22: Wednesday, July 29, 2026

## Day 23: Thursday, July 30, 2026

- Flight back from Hawaii. Wrote a long letter to myself to open at the end of the noncompete — about two thousand words, written on the plane.
- Flights locked on the Japan side too: SFO→Tokyo on Aug 24, Tokyo→Sapporo on Aug 29. Four nights in Tokyo, then a buffer day in Sapporo before the first guided day.

## Day 24: Friday, July 31, 2026

- Landed back in NYC around midday. Eight days until the flight west, and the apartment has to be empty before it.
- Booked Stuf for Aug 3 move-in.
- Made the move-out real: leaving the LIC apartment, and everything goes into an NYC storage unit rather than getting shipped to my parents.
- Killed the valet-storage idea, which had been the appealing one. My building wants a certificate of insurance from any moving company plus a reserved freight elevator, filed in advance, and there's no lead time for that. So every box gets hand-carried to the storage unit by me.
- Decided not to renew the California license before the trip. An international permit is a translation of one specific license and goes invalid the moment that license is renewed — and AAA only issues them inside the US, so it would be unfixable from Hokkaido.
- Money plan for Japan: Wise for paying guides in yen, Schwab checking for rural cash.
- Found out REI SoHo closed permanently on the 23rd. There's no REI left in the metro area, so every pack try-on moves to the Bay Area.
- Replied to Yoshi, Dylan and Kevin.

## Day 25: Saturday, August 1, 2026

- A correspondence day wedged into the middle of a move-out. Dylan came back into contact, and at this point he isn't really a guide relationship — more someone who lives there.
- He volunteered himemasu, landlocked sockeye, unprompted: peaks mid-September into October, males go humped and vivid red. Kussharo peaks around mid-October, which is exactly when my eastern block runs. It's the first fish I've named as a personal goal instead of accepting a guide's recommendation.
- Shin's block firmed up — free from Oct 8, offered to arrange lodging Oct 8–18. Asked him for seven or eight fishing days with rest woven between them.
- Decided to skip Schwab. It's a brokerage application rather than a bank one, the timing is tight against Aug 24, and the entire prize is $50–90 of ATM fees across three months.
