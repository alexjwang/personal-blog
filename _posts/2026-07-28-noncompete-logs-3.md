# logs

I'm backfilling most of these entries on August 11, 2026. I use Claude hooked up to my Obsidian (and a stored knowledge base) for a lot of my day to day decision making, and I've noticed it has a surprisingly good memory as a result. I've backfilled a lot of bullet points from its knowledge. So the rule for this post: the bullet points are Claude's, written from my notes. The paragraphs are mine.

## Day 15: Wednesday, July 22, 2026

- Kevin sent his actual September/October calendars as screenshots. His open days turned out much broader than his emails had implied — he's free during most of Harry's Niseko week, and free at the very end of October, both of which I'd written off.
- Harry applying pressure: other guests were asking about the week he'd been holding for me. That forced the Niseko decision.
- Yoshi (Lucky Field) replied — likes the shape of 6 guided days with a rolling rest day, needs check-in/out dates before he can price it, 50% deposit to confirm.
- Messaged Shin on WhatsApp asking whether the eastern block could push to Oct 13+, to put a rest gap between Tokachi and the east.
- Rebun Island came back into the plan, first as a finale. The reasoning: a return trip is genuinely uncertain, so deferring Rebun is closer to giving it up than to postponing it.
- Started talking myself toward "fewer guides, go deeper" — concentrating days on Oku rather than spreading them across five people.

## Day 16: Thursday, July 23, 2026

- Kevin replied twice in one day. Akan took shape (leave Niseko the 17th, fish the 18th and 19th, back to Sapporo the 20th), the Shikaribetsu trip turned out to be a genuine invitation rather than a brush-off, and he offered Sapporo single days Aug 30–Sep 5.
- Moved Rebun from a late-October finale to early September. Better weather, no cross-island backtrack, and it frees the back half of the trip.
- Kevin argued for October over September on Rebun — iwana drop downstream as it cools, so 30–40cm fish instead of 10–20cm. Went September anyway. Small fish in tight headwaters is the actual thing I want.
- The best thing he said, unprompted: do solo days. Pick a river nobody's shown you, research the access and the entry yourself, expect it to take a few attempts to crack. That's the literal point of the fifteen months.
- Started leaning toward releasing Takeshi.

## Day 17: Friday, July 24, 2026

- Harry sent the first invoice of the trip — the first real money. Checked it against the shortened three-day block before paying, since he might have invoiced off the original seven days.
- Paid Kevin's deposit. It turned out to be ¥10,000 per day rather than a percentage, so ¥30,000 for three days. Aug 31, Sep 2 and Sep 4 are locked.
- Oku replied: a cancellation opened Sep 22–26 and he recommends the 24th–26th. Camping is on — a private creek-side site, no showers.
- Learned about Silver Week (Sep 19–23) from Oku, which immediately blew up yesterday's Akan plan. Kevin's verdict when I passed it on: the long weekend he'd avoid at all costs, weekend pressure doubled on every river.
- Kevin costed Rebun at Sep 7–10, about ¥289,000. His breakdown had a dropped zero — ¥16,500 where it should have said ¥165,000 — so his own total came out ¥148,500 low. Flagged it politely, which is a strange thing to have to do.
- Built the full cost model for the first time: roughly ¥3.53M, about $23,900. That's $6k over the ceiling I'd set.
- Decided the Tokachi week: the lodge at Lucky Field, Sep 27–Oct 3, five guided days.
- Cash research. Guides want cash on the day, which adds up to ¥800k–1M across the trip. 7-Eleven ATMs take foreign cards 24/7 at ¥100k a transaction; Japan Post is everywhere but caps at ¥50k and keeps banking hours.

## Day 18: Saturday, July 25, 2026

- Flights booked. Depart Aug 24, land in Japan Aug 25. The start of the trip is a fixed point now instead of a range.
- Lucky Field quoted ¥496,000 all-in — six nights plus five guided days — under the number I'd decided I'd walk at. Sent the 50% deposit, ¥248,000, by Wise. The guide finally has a name: Koudai Araki.
- Shin said yes. He and his brother both cover the block, he's flexible on dates, Oct 8–16.
- Released Takeshi. It would have been the third block in the same region, I'd have ~31 guided days behind me by then, and I'd be far too tired to absorb elite technical instruction. Sent a warm apology and meant it.
- Rebun lodging solved. Hana Rebun was down to one room at ¥50,000 a night, so Onko Chishin instead at about ¥17,000 — same street, and Kevin is staying there too. He booked the hotel and the ferry himself. Rebun fixed at Sep 6–9.
- Four of five guides now locked with money down. Only Shin's dates left open in the entire trip.

## Day 19: Sunday, July 26, 2026

There's no record Claude has of this day, and that's because I went fishing with Sophia and her family! I booked a fishing charter boat to fit the 3 of us. I honestly was thinking that the style of fishing might be a touch too difficult for the two of them (jigging), and it did end up being quite physically difficult. I was happy to enjoy a bit of success and catch some amberjack. Honestly, a really sick day fishing, and I ended up with a Croc tan which was pretty funny.
