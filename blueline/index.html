        River

          This is a retro-style idle game I built while fly fishing in Hokkaido. The mechanics are all based on real life (how the current moves, where fish sit in it, how they use cover), and every fish and bug in it is one I've come across firsthand in Hokkaido. I have a version of this for my personal use as a productivity tool, hooked up to Obsidian and other productivity apps, but I figured the game portion could stand on its own here. Pick a bug, click the water to drop it, and see who comes up for it.
