Himemasu at Lake Kussharo, with tons of salmon right at our feet: hooked jaws, bright red, crazy humps. They don't eat while they're spawning, so you flash a big streamer past their face to trigger an aggression strike. Three fish from literally hundreds.

> Retrieval speed keeps the depth: the streamer has to sit at eye level and still move fast enough to make them angry.

Dinner at Teshikaga Ramen. The best ramen of my life, and I thought I was a miso ramen hater.
