Day 100. Wind made casting hard and the cold water meant few active fish, so we moved spots and ended up back on the river from the first day.

At the last spot I read a seam that looked like it could hold one. It didn't feel big at first, until the fish woke up. A long fight with a giant rainbow.

> I'd made my peace with not catching much, and was just enjoying the cold water running past my legs. That fish brought the spark back: the fun is in the challenge.
