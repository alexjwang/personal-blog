Rugged coastline, cliffs, dense green, and streams you could step across. The fishing was bluelining: follow the thin blue lines on the map into water nobody bothers with, and look for wild iwana.

These iwana were remote enough such that they are a different variant from those on Hokkaido main island. They have very different colors and are super pretty!

> Closer to the fishing I imagined back in July than any guided day has been.
