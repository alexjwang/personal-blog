Second day with Kevin, this was somewhat unexpectedly a super productive day. After the first day, I didn't have super high expectations for fishing in Hokkaido, but I ended up catching two large rainbow trout super early in the day! We were mostly nymphing here, and the wading was super tough (ended up being the toughest wading in all of Hokkaido). The river had these big rocks as the river bottom, and walking often required somehow finding traction on slippery, sloped surfaces.
