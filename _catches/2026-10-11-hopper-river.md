Maybe 20 rainbows, 8 of them pretty big. Apparently most people catch one big one here. Wild fish deep upstream in the mountains, catchable for only a week or two in autumn, and exceptionally beautiful.

Big hoppers so the fish could find the fly through the leaves, and mostly long backhand casts under the cover. Roll casting is still a weakness, and I think my line is a touch too heavy for my rod.

> Shin didn't think there were fish in a run of riffles into a long deep channel. It had multiple seams, so I fished it anyway: four giant rainbows. My own read of the water, independent of the guide.
