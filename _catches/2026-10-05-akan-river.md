Four fishermen lined up one run after another, because there were big rainbows visible all through the water. We waded around a bend and had it to ourselves.

First rainbow came nymphing, around 35cm. Then a 40cm+ out of a deep pool in the middle of the river. Further up, several giant fish were stacked in soft water at the top of a run. On a dry-dropper (grasshopper on top, nymph below) I cast just ahead of one and watched it come up and eat the dry.

> Extra satisfying on the new rod and reel, and on a dry fly.

Mid-afternoon, a sound like an old man grunting through pull-ups turned out to be a brown bear. My guide looked for five seconds, said "yup that's a bear", and we walked away briskly.
