We started at a stream in the middle of a farm field, it was surprisingly straight, clear, fast and cold. I thought maybe it was manmade but apparently natural? Tons of long overhanging grass, I had my first run in with stinging nettle on the way down the steep bank into the water. Learned later this is perfect brown trout habitat. Wasn’t sure what to expect fishing wise since by now I’d had good/bad guides, and Oku is known to be a master. But sure enough, caught tons of nice browns here, had lots of fun. 

> New word, learned the hard way: irakusa. Stinging nettle.
