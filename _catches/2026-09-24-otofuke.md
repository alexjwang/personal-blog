First day with Oku. That first run opened my eyes to how good a run can be: bites left and right the whole way down. My first fish he called "a small one", and a few seconds later I was putting it on the reel. All the "small" fish he'd been pointing out were actually decent.

> Three Japanese cranes flew over together. Stood there awestruck for a few minutes.

Finished up north near Ashoro, at a second spot under steep cliffs, where the wind made casting much harder than I expected.
