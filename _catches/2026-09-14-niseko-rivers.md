Pretty slippery river, definitely was a bit slower on wading here. The river itself was pretty, but fishing wise wasn’t anything super spectacular. Saw lots of fish but was hard to catch a lot of them. Dry dropper rig, the dry fly was surprisingly more effective. Had a nice brown that I missed because reaction was too slow. Big heavy double nymph rigs were hard to cast, had to be quite close but worried about spooking. Also had bobber on so made it harder to finesse and control fishing. Nothing too big, lost the only nice one (rainbow).
