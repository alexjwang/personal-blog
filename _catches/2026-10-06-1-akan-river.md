The best day of fishing of all time.

Took the indicator off. The current ran fast into a deep pocket and crashed into a 90 degree bend, bouncing back off the far bank into a second current. Where the two met there was slack water, at the deepest part of the pool, right by a steep dropoff. I let the nymph ride the main current, bounce with the ricochet, and settle into the slack. The line straightened ever so slightly and stopped.

> Sixty centimeters. I could not have caught that fish even a few days ago.

Four more giant rainbows from the same spot, each one 50cm+ and a personal best on any other day. Downriver, an iwana of about 30cm (a shaku, a trophy for iwana), then two more rainbows that fought hard for their size.
