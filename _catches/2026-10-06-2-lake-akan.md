Practiced streamers off a sandy slope at the river mouth, where the sand drops into nothing. One step too far and I nearly slid in.

Then a fish jumped clear out of the water, its whole body in the air, utterly massive. I chased the jumps with a dry and then with the Alexandra, a streamer Oku gave me because the name sort of matched mine. Hooked a nice fish, and as it came in we could see it wasn't a rainbow.

> Amemasu, the last species on my list. Standing in the lake with giant fish jumping all around and the mountains behind them.
