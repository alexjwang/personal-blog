The first spot was blown out: the dams opened after the rain, trucks drove around with speakerphones warning people off, and the sirens are a scary sound.

Below a dam the water comes out fast and rough, then slows into a deep, still, clear gorge with giant rainbows in it. Sight fishing: we stood hidden in the rocks for 30 or 40 minutes scouting risers and planning the cast. The cast wasn't great but it worked, and it was a 45cm fish with awesome colours.

> Caught a fish on a fly I tied. Something I made fooled a trout into thinking it was a real bug.
