When we pulled up, there was enough wind where tons of fish were rising. But right when I started fishing, the wind stopped and the surface went glass still and there was no more rising. Swapped to Alexandra wet fly and it worked really well, but I lost two nice fish when reaching for camera record button. Now, after fishing for longer, I realize that I probably was not maintaining enough bend in the rod to keep these fish hooked, I should focus harder on that to land fish. Wind eventually picked up again but no luck, bite stopped at some point and we moved to second spot. Lake was really pretty though!
