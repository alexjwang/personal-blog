On this last day, we were driving back to Obihiro and Oku stopped along a small creek deep in the mountain forest so I could go for some Dolly Varden. I heard these fish are quite rare in Hokkaido, so I hadn’t really expected to catch one (lots of people have never caught before). He put me in a spot where I caught nearly a dozen. I had a ton of fun fishing dry flies here, the fish were the prettiest I’d seen in Hokkaido, but also the scenery was amazing. Just small creek in mossy wooded forest. I had a ton of fun. 

> Probably the prettiest fish I've seen in Hokkaido.
