On the way back to Obihiro, Oku took me to a small creek deep in the mountain forest. I'd heard dolly varden were rare and plenty of people who fish Hokkaido never catch one. He put me in a spot where I caught nearly a dozen.

> Probably the prettiest fish I've seen in Hokkaido.
